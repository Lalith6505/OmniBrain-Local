1. Sovereign Architecture: 100% On-Device Neural Execution

OmniBrain Local is architected from foundational mathematical principles to function entirely offline on your physical hardware. Unlike cloud-hosted AI chatbots that forward your messages to remote server clusters, OmniBrain Local executes neural network model weights directly on your smartphone's NPU, GPU, and CPU. No prompts, model weights, vector embeddings, tool outputs, or conversation transcripts are ever transmitted across the internet.

2. Zero Cloud API Keys & No Telemetry

The application requires no OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or third-party server API keys. It contains zero analytics software, zero behavioral telemetry libraries, zero diagnostic crash-reporting daemons, and zero commercial tracking SDKs. It operates without an internet connection and will function indefinitely in Airplane Mode.

3. Local Document & Vector Storage

All documents indexed into the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) knowledge base are processed locally. Generated 384-dimensional dense vectors and SQLite database tables reside within the application's private, encrypted internal sandbox. You maintain exclusive sovereignty and ownership over your data, with complete freedom to export or erase records at your discretion.

4. License Integrity

OmniBrain Local is distributed exclusively as a one-time paid commercial license on the Google Play Store. It features zero in-app purchases, zero recurrent subscription fees, and zero remote DRM check-ins.

5. Inquiries

For questions or technical verifications regarding OmniBrain Local's offline architecture, please contact the development team through the official Google Play Console developer portal.
